How to read this

Every proposition here has two layers.

The split matters commercially. The basic layer wins the account and must be cheap to run. The value-add layer earns the margin and makes leaving expensive.

Basic proposition

What a bank has to do, done without the friction.

A real local IBAN, a card, instant payments, direct debits, bills, cash access, deposit protection.

Value-add proposition

What most banks don't do, or charge a lot for.

Multi-currency, Buy Now Pay Later, Marketplace, Loyalty, FX remittance, Investing, Open-banking aggregation, embedded finance.

Onboarding

From stranger to customer in one sitting.

STEP 1

Identify once

National eID, the EU Digital Identity wallet or a video session. KYC, AML screening and the affordability check run in the same flow.

STEP 2

Account and card go live

A local IBAN and a virtual card before the screen closes. The physical card follows by post; the credit limit is already assessed.

STEP 3

The shelf opens gradually

Instalments, savings, investing and overdraft unlock on behaviour rather than on a new application each time.
